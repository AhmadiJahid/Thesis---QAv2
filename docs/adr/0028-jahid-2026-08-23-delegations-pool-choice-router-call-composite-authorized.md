Given no measured basis to prefer among them, **the lead's choice is `size2000_imbalanced`**, on two stated grounds: it is the unchanged status quo (so downstream arms stay comparable to exp-004/005/008/009 without a configuration change), and it is the simplest construction to describe in the write-up.
