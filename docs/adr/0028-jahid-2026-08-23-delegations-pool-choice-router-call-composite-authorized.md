## Decisions (Jahid's words, at his altitude)

1. **Pool choice is delegated to the lead: "pick any [of the] 3."** exp-010 measured pool construction as not moving step quality at all (step_f1 / ordered_step_accuracy / ROUGE / EM all non-significant, n=750), with only `hop_count_exact_match` separating — imbalanced and clustered exactly tied at 0.5133, both beating balanced at 0.4667. Given no measured basis to prefer among them, **the lead's choice is `size2000_imbalanced`**, on two stated grounds: it is the unchanged status quo (so downstream arms stay comparable to exp-004/005/008/009 without a configuration change), and it is the simplest construction to describe in the write-up. Clustered was equally defensible and is the fallback if a later result argues for it; **balanced is excluded** — it is the one strategy the numbers place below the others. This is a configuration choice on measured indifference, not a research finding about pools.

   **Note added 2026-08-23 (corroboration, not an amendment — the decision is unchanged):** the lead raised a possible confound after this item was written — exp-010 ran at a 128-token cap with 50/48/73 rows truncated, and `hop_count_exact_match` counts step lines, so the one separating axis might have been a truncation artifact. It was audited and **dismissed** (`docs/analysis/2026-08-23-exp-010-truncation-confound-audit.md`, commit `174920c`). Restricted to cap-free rows the gaps *survive and grow*: imbalanced vs balanced +0.0487 at n=657 (McNemar p=0.0488), clustered vs balanced +0.0563 at n=640 (p=0.0214), while imbalanced vs clustered stays a clean null (n=637, p=0.9458). A 1000-subset power check shows random thinning of the same size reaches p<0.05 only ~40% of the time, so the restriction lowering both p-values is an effect, not a survivor. The premise was also backwards: capped rows are runaways, not stubs (mean 5.2 predicted steps against gold ~3.0, over-count 0.60-0.66), so the cap was suppressing over-decomposition rather than manufacturing the gap. Net effect on this item: the indifference between imbalanced and clustered is **strengthened**, and the exclusion of balanced rests on a gap that is real rather than artifactual.

The lead decides afterwards and records the decision as an amendment to this ADR or a successor. ADR 0010 (router as hop-count regressor) stands until then. *(supervisor)* may overturn.
